Key idea: two tiles 480 apart (centres 0 and 480), each 520 wide so they overlap 40px. Tile at centre 0 covers −260…260 = the whole stage plus a little; tile at centre 480 covers 220…740 = parked off the right, waiting. Both slide left together. When a tile's centre passes −480 it jumps ahead of its partner (change x by 960) and becomes the new "parked" tile. Endless belt.

Why 520 and not 480? Scratch fences every sprite — it never lets one slide fully off the stage, keeping ~15px on. So an exactly-480 tile's centre could never reach −480 and the wrap would never fire. A 520 tile can (its centre reaches −485 before the fence bites), and the 40px overlap hides the seam. Flip wrap rule → set x to 480: with the 40px cushion it still holds at normal speeds — set x just keeps a little less overlap, because it throws away the fence overshoot that change x carries. Crank the speed slider up and both eventually tear (set x a touch sooner). Prefer change x by 960 anyway: it needs no fixed 480 and keeps working with more tiles, other widths, or a changing speed.
